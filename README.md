# LOTG Labs Workbench — Technical Records

Public technical records of selected LOTG Labs investigations: what was tested, what happened, what was learned, and what remains to be tested.

These records complement readable [LOTG Labs public artifacts](https://labs.lilaonthegrid.com/artifacts/). They preserve technical investigation context rather than duplicating website articles. Research domains remain distinct from their technical research home and publication venue.

## Record pattern

Each record preserves its identifier and documents its status, origin, operating conditions, question, baseline, changed conditions, observations, canaries, retests, intervention, result, interpretation, limitations, and next validation step. Link related public artifacts near the top. Keep observations distinct from working hypotheses and untested proposals.

Records are stored in `records/` under their stable identifiers. Publication here does not imply that a provisional finding has become a validated standard operating procedure.

## Publication boundary

This repository contains intentionally released technical documentation. Private bridge implementations, local filesystem paths, scripts, configuration, credentials, unreleased production details, and internal production methods are excluded.
